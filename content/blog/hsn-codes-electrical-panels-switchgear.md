Every export shipment of electrical equipment from India carries an 8-digit HSN code on the invoice and shipping bill. The first six digits are the same worldwide. The last two are India's own, and the importing country's customs will use its own national digits after the first six. A wrong code delays clearance, so it is worth getting right before the quote goes out.

## How the 8537 heading works

Heading 8537 covers boards, panels, consoles and cabinets fitted with two or more switching or protection devices. India splits it by voltage only:

| Product | HSN (India) | Covers |
| --- | --- | --- |
| LT panels: PCC, MCC, APFC, ATS, PLC, DBs | 85371000 | boards for 1000 V or less |
| MV switchgear panels, RMUs, GIS bays | 85372000 | boards for more than 1000 V |

There is no separate line for MCC, PLC or capacitor panels. They all sit in 85371000.

## Breakers and switching devices

Loose devices are classified by voltage and type:

| Product | HSN (India) |
| --- | --- |
| Air circuit breaker (ACB), 1000 V or less | 85362010 |
| Moulded case breaker (MCCB) | 85362020 |
| Miniature breaker (MCB) | 85362030 |
| RCCB, RCBO, ELCB | 85362040 |
| Relays above 60 V; contactors are commonly declared here | 85364900 |
| Vacuum circuit breaker, 11 kV | 85352121 |
| Vacuum circuit breaker, 33 kV | 85352122 |
| Isolators and load break switches above 1000 V | 85353090 |

## Enclosures and parts

Empty cabinets are not panels until they are fitted, so they go under 8538:

| Product | HSN (India) |
| --- | --- |
| Unfitted panel cabinets for industrial use | 85381010 |
| Other unfitted boards and cabinets | 85381090 |
| Parts for 8535 to 8537 (busbars, mechanisms, shutters) | 85389000 |
| Junction boxes, 1000 V or less | 85369030 |

## Common mistakes

- **Using 85371090.** It does not exist in the Indian tariff. Use 85371000.
- **Declaring a complete RMU under 8535.** A finished RMU is a board with several devices, so it belongs in 85372000. Loose switching modules can go to 85359030.
- **Splitting a panel into its parts.** A complete, wired panel ships under 8537 even if it contains breakers from 8536.
- **Copying the importing country's code onto the Indian invoice.** The first six digits match; the last two do not.
