The same MCC panel can clear customs in Dubai and sit for weeks in Lagos, only because a certificate is missing. Each destination has its own conformity scheme on top of the technical standard. Here is what the main markets ask for. Where the certificate is issued per shipment, GoodBiz Power Systems arranges it so it travels with the equipment; where it belongs to the manufacturer, we source equipment that already carries it.

## The Gulf

| Country | Typical standard | Certificate or approval |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Saudi Arabia | IEC 61439 · IEC 62271 · SEC specifications | SASO certificate through SABER |
| United Arab Emirates | IEC 61439 · DEWA or ADDC regulations | utility approval list, ECAS where the product is listed |
| Qatar | IEC 61439 · KAHRAMAA regulations | KAHRAMAA approval |
| Oman | IEC · OES specifications | distribution company approval |
| Kuwait | IEC · MEW specifications | MEW approval |

Gulf sites also expect equipment rated for a 50 °C ambient (55 °C in Kuwait), and Saudi Arabia and Kuwait ask for nameplates in Arabic and English. Utility approvals in the Gulf are given to the manufacturer for each model, so the make has to be on the utility's list before it is ordered; the SASO certificate in Saudi Arabia is the one that is arranged per shipment.

## Africa

| Country | Typical standard | Certificate |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Nigeria | IEC · NIS | SONCAP certificate |
| Kenya | IEC · KS | PVoC certificate of conformity |
| Tanzania | IEC · TZS | PVoC certificate of conformity |
| South Africa | SANS / IEC 61439 | NRCS where applicable |
| Ghana | IEC · GS | GSA conformity |

## Other markets

| Country | Typical standard | Certificate |
| --- | --- | --- |
| United States | UL 891 · UL 508A · IEEE C37 | UL listing or field evaluation |
| Australia | AS/NZS 61439 | RCM |
| United Kingdom | BS EN 61439 · BS EN 62271 | UKCA |

UL listing and UKCA marking belong to the manufacturer, and RCM is registered by the Australian supplier, so for these markets we source equipment that already carries the mark.

## What to send us

The certificate is built from the technical file, so the earlier we know the destination, the smoother it goes. Include:

- the destination country and the utility or consultant approving the design
- the single-line diagram and the rating of each item
- any approved vendor list that names breaker or relay brands

## Typical values, your specification first

The tables above are the usual requirements for each country. A project specification or a utility's approval list can add to them, and it always takes priority.
